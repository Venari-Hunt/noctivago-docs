Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-24. This covers the Noctívago Android app. The Windows app has its own answer in the FAQ.

What the app stores

Your mixes, sounds, recordings and settings are stored on your phone only. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and the app has no analytics or telemetry. Removing the app deletes everything it stored.

When the app uses the internet

  • Browse Sounds: the words you search for are sent to freesound.org, and the sound you add is downloaded from it.
  • Community tab: the app asks the Community server for a list of shared mixes and downloads the one you pick. Nothing about you is sent beyond the request itself.
  • Links you type in: the app downloads the sound at that address.
  • Updates: the app checks for a newer version and downloads it. You can turn automatic install off in Settings.

Turn off Browse Sounds and Community in Settings and the app stops using them.

Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only for the Record button in the Sounds tab. It records only while you hold a recording open, and the recording stays on your phone.

Error log

Errors are kept in a small log on your phone. It is never sent anywhere; Settings has a button to share it by hand if you want help with a problem.

Contact
