What's new

The 15 most recent releases, newest first. The app updates itself; see the full changelog for every release since the first one.

v0.1.268 — Export for phone

  • Getting ready for the Noctívago phone app: Export can now save a preset "For phone". Pick it in the Export tab's Format list (needs Export plugin 1.1.0). It writes a preset file with every sound's Remix settings and all reverb already baked in, so the phone only has to mix. On the Beach preset: 18 sounds in 27 seconds, a 7 MB file. A loop longer than 10 minutes ships as its middle 15 seconds, and the result warns you when a preset may need too much memory for a phone.

v0.1.267 — Auto-started sleep timer asks first

  • When the sleep timer is set to start every time Noctívago opens, it now tells you first. A notice says what the timer will do and how long it is, and counts down 20 seconds before starting. "Don't start" skips it this time; "Start now" starts it right away.

v0.1.266 — Only one copy of the app opens

  • Opening Noctívago while it's already running now brings back the open window instead of starting a second copy. This covers clicking the pinned taskbar icon while the app sits in the tray or is minimized.

v0.1.265 — Sleep timer can start on its own

  • New in the sleep timer: "Start this timer every time Noctívago opens." Tick it once and every launch starts a timer with the last minutes (or time of day) and action you used, with no need to open the timer. Untick it in the same place to stop. A timer still running from before you closed the app is resumed instead of being replaced.

v0.1.264 — Faster exports with pitched sounds

  • Exports with Random Interval or Scheduled sounds that change pitch each play are much faster. Each pitch is now prepared once and reused, instead of once for every single play. On a 1-hour beach mix the whole export went from 71 seconds to 43; long exports gain much more. Pitches can differ from before by a tiny, inaudible amount.
  • Long Opus exports no longer slow down near the end. The last part of a 9-hour export used to re-read hours of each looping sound before mixing it. The result is identical.

v0.1.263 — Faster Opus exports

  • With "Faster export" on, saving to Opus mixes and compresses several stretches of the export at once instead of one long pass. On a 1-hour beach mix the last step went from 43 seconds to about 20; longer exports gain more. The result sounds the same.

v0.1.260 — Faster long exports

  • Long exports are faster again. Sounds that play now and then are combined with much less wasted work, and saving to MP3, Opus, FLAC or Ogg now happens while the mix is still being made instead of after it. A 9-hour beach mix went from about 19 minutes to under 16 (a 1-hour version: 2:22 to 1:33). The result sounds the same.

v0.1.259 — A title bar that matches the app

  • The window has its own dark title bar now instead of the plain Windows one: the app icon and name on the left, and minimize, maximize and close in matching colors on the right. Dragging, double-click to maximize and Windows snap layouts work as before.

v0.1.258 — Faster exports for sounds that play rarely

  • Exports are much faster when a Random Interval or Scheduled sound plays only now and then. A sound playing every few minutes across a long export used to be prepared as one piece as long as the whole export, which could take many minutes on its own. On a test 9-hour beach mix, the whole export went from 13.5 minutes to just over 2. The result sounds the same.

v0.1.257 — Much faster YouTube imports

  • Adding a sound from YouTube is now dozens of times faster. Ten minutes of an 8-hour rain video used to take about 5 minutes to download; now it takes around 10 to 15 seconds. Works in Add from link and Browse Sounds. If the fast way doesn't work for a link, the app quietly uses the old one.

v0.1.256 — Faster community uploads

  • Sharing a preset to the community compresses several sounds at once, so the compressing step is about 3 times faster on a preset with many big sounds (12 large files went from about 7 minutes to 2).

v0.1.255 — Cut long sounds when a shared preset is too large

  • When a preset is too large to share, each long sound in the size list gets a "Cut to 15 s" button. Click it and the upload keeps only 15 seconds from the middle of the part the preset plays; the list shows the new size right away. Your own copy of the sound is not changed. Needs Community plugin 1.2.0.

v0.1.254 — Pinned taskbar icon stays visible after updates

  • Updating no longer leaves a pinned taskbar icon blank. The installer now tells Windows to refresh its icons, so a pinned Noctívago shows the current icon right away (this bit some people after the v0.1.251 icon change).

v0.1.253 — See what a community upload is doing

  • Sharing a preset to the community now shows a progress bar that runs through compressing each sound and uploading, instead of only a line of text.
  • You see each sound's size before and after compression, so you know what the upload weighs.
  • When a preset is too large to share, the message names the heaviest sounds and highlights them in the list, so you know which ones to shorten or remove.

v0.1.252 — Icons in the right-click menu

  • Every option in a sound's right-click menu now has an icon (play, solo, mute, rename, tags, folder, trash and so on), so you can spot the one you want at a glance. Presets and Sound Groups that already hold the sound show a check mark.
